01THE STORY

ORBITAL HISTORY

A horizon of its own

Ofek (אופק, “horizon”) is a series of Israeli satellites developed for Earth observation. The program is led by Israel’s Ministry of Defense, with Israel Aerospace Industries as a key developer.

The first satellite, Ofek 1, launched on 19 September 1988 aboard the Shavit launch vehicle from Israel. It was a technology demonstrator, designed to test systems such as power generation, communications, and satellite operation in orbit.

The early missions built the foundation for more advanced observation satellites. Ofek 3, launched in 1995, marked a major step in the series’ imaging capabilities. Later generations continued to improve the technology used to collect and transmit images of Earth.

Most Ofek satellites operate in low Earth orbit. Their development reflects Israel’s effort to build and maintain independent space capabilities, from spacecraft design to launch and ground control.
